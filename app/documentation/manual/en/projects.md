## Projects and tools {#projects}

CyCode can open supported workspaces, `.sln` solutions and Unreal descriptors. CMake/MSBuild, Cargo, npm, JVM and shaders have distinct workflows and prerequisites: read the specialized guide before executing. Tool discovery does not establish acceptance of every scenario. Native owners retain targets, arguments, output and cancellation.

## Unreal {#unreal}

Check engine version, project, targets and analysis data. Build, Live Coding and automation have different prerequisites. Unreal, UnrealLaunch and UnrealAutomation preserve their limits and validation status.
